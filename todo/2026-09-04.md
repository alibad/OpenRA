# 2026-09-04

## Experiences load compatible gameplay and describe their actual effects

- Selecting a module now resolves the whole dependency chain and handles
  conflicts in either direction. Incompatible modules and their dependent
  extensions are removed together; an impossible dependency graph is rejected
  before loading gameplay. Shared internal foundations unload when their final
  faction or module is disabled. Dependency files load before their overrides.
- Turning off a module now stops its saved parameter overrides from changing
  mission-authored actors. The saved values remain available when it is enabled
  again. Local validation can explicitly choose a preset without a user's saved
  custom loadout silently overriding the requested verification profile.
- The menu and match announcement count modern factions and gameplay modules
  separately. Hidden foundations and authoring contracts no longer inflate the
  capability count. Custom selections display as Custom experience.
- Genuine authoring contracts remain available under Tools & Compatibility and
  are explicitly labelled as requiring compatible content. They do not claim
  to add an immediate skirmish mechanic. Existing user selections are preserved.
- Corrected misleading cards for implemented abilities: rocket-soldier barrage,
  APC rapid transit, Spy temporary control, Artillery/Tesla additional weapons,
  commander bonuses and China's carrier wing now describe their real users,
  controls and limits. The generic naval-role dependency no longer silently
  enables unrelated Artillery/Tesla upgrades when a faction alone is selected.
  China explicitly includes its actual carrier-wing dependency.

## AI observations include secondary weapons and the current attack mode

- Units and defense buildings report air/surface targeting from all active
  weapons, including secondary anti-air guns. The previous implementation only
  inspected the first armament and missed weapons restricted to Water or
  Structure targets, such as coastal missiles.
- Disabled/deployed attack modes, paused attacks and inactive armaments are
  excluded. Switching a portable unit between AT and AA mode therefore updates
  the capabilities seen by the companion. Public mod target definitions supply
  the domain mapping, including custom target types and explicit exclusions;
  this does not inspect hidden enemy actors.
- The existing ground flag is a broad non-air capability, including naval and
  underwater targets; it is not a promise to hit every ground target. The
  single-weapon name/reload fields continue to describe the first active weapon.
- Seven new targeting test cases cover secondary AA, Water/Structure/Underwater
  targets, invalid-target precedence, custom drone targets and mode changes.
  Actual RA2 snapshots and gameplay checks belong to the product validation.

## Doctrine AI keeps useful production queues moving

- Role-based production now counts recruited units even when their actor IDs
  are not in the original actor-specific weight list. Their existing combat
  roles and unit limits therefore contribute to future production choices.
  A module can also recruit through role shares without a separate ID list.
- Aircraft without an available rearm slot are filtered before the production
  choice, so one preferred but unavailable plane cannot repeatedly prevent
  another usable aircraft from being queued. The existing rearm-slot policy is
  preserved. Zero production weights do not recruit unwanted actors.
- Six new production-selection tests exercise missing-role replacement,
  role-only indexing and limits, tech delays, unavailable-aircraft fallback in
  role and actor modes, and zero weights. Native from-MCV faction opening tests
  are recorded independently by the product repository.
- Role-only tracking includes buildable unit queues, not defense buildings or
  script-only actors sharing a role tag. Explicit transformed/deployed actor
  entries remain tracked.
