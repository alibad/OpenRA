# 2026-09-02

- Automatic AI selection is now the primary model-setting control, with setup
  size and voice readiness visible. Technical models/providers and optional
  LM Studio discovery live under Advanced.
- The main menu shows the current Experience, modern factions, and enabled
  capability count, with a direct Factions & Capabilities button. Matches also
  announce the active experience without opening a modal.

## What We Fixed

- Reset restores local transcription and spoken replies instead of cloud
  defaults. Profile changes accurately report that a relaunch is needed.
- Regression coverage checks the actual shipped World War III profile includes
  all five modern factions and enhanced mechanics, not just its setting name.

## Key Decisions

- Preserve explicit player-selected experiences. Fresh installs retain the
  enhanced World War III default. Do not label its original/reusable mechanics
  as bundled Red Alert 2 units or artwork.
