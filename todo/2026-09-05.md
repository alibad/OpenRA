# 2026-09-05

## Native doctrine AI recruits specialists across independent production queues

- Fixed a live RA2 AUTO failure where Iran could spend 50,000 test credits on
  infantry, ships and ground vehicles without recruiting its first aircraft.
  The vehicle factory compared its tank/AA/artillery/drone roles with the entire
  army, so independently growing infantry and naval queues kept tanks
  permanently deficient and starved lower-weight specialist roles.
- Role composition is now balanced against matching owned roles recruitable
  in the current production queue. Available role weights are deduplicated and
  normalized with integer cross-multiplication; scaling all weights preserves
  the same choice. Actor-specific legacy production shares are unchanged.
- Delays, unit limits and rearm availability are applied before role selection.
  A temporarily unavailable role does not dilute the current queue's targets.
  Capped actor variants still count when another recruitable actor fills the
  same role. Multi-queue actors count once in each relevant queue calculation;
  explicitly tracked transformed actors without Buildable metadata retain
  their matching-role contribution.
