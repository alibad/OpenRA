# 2026-09-28

## Native Faction Catalog

### What We Built

- The main menu now has a **Factions** entry, and each faction pack in
  Factions & Capabilities has an **Open in Faction Catalog** button. Both open
  a native catalog of the loaded game (Classic and RA2) that lists only that
  mode's factions: modern factions first, then the original countries, with a
  note naming catalog factions that exist only in another mode.
- For each faction it shows:
  - the title, tagline, doctrine, summary and signature units;
  - the roster grouped by domain, with production icons and tags for signature
    and unique units;
  - unit details read from the loaded rules: cost, build time, HP, armor,
    speed, sight, power, transport, attack reach, roles, counters,
    prerequisites and each weapon's range, damage, reload and targets.
- "More on the web" and "Unit on the web" open the public
  `rtsai.net/{factions|units}/{id}?mode=` pages through `Renderer.TryOpenUrl`.
- Keyboard:
  - Left/Right: faction.
  - Up/Down: unit.
  - PageUp/PageDown: details.
  - Enter: web page.
  - Esc: back.
- The panel is 1200×680 at 1280×720 and 1440×900 at 1920×1080. Long text
  uses native multiline tooltips and scroll panels. `docs/faction-catalog.md`
  has the code map and the catalog location contract.
- `OpenRA.Utility <mod> --check-faction-catalog` validates the shared catalog
  against the loaded rules and Experience faction packs. It can also export the
  rules digest that the main menu, the catalog and the in-match companion HUD
  post to the companion.

### What We Fixed

- Saudi Arabia's roster was missing its fixed-wing airfield `SAFLD`, which only
  Saudi Arabia can build. The new check found it.

### Key Decisions

- Every number comes from the loaded rules, and rosters and exclusivity come
  from a static reading of the tech tree. The product catalog is editorial
  only.
- The panel ships in common chrome but appears only where a mod declares it.
  Other mods that share the common main menu hide the button and keep their
  layout.
- Air reach is judged against both Classic `AirborneActor` and RA2 `Air`
  target profiles. The existing observation classifier, `ObservationWeaponTargets`,
  only recognizes the literal `Air`. That classifier is not changed here; see
  the product report.

### Verification

- Release solution build: zero warnings and zero errors.
- Tests: 576 passed, 2 skipped. That is the 544-test baseline plus 32
  `FactionCatalogTest` cases covering the tech-tree reader, prerequisite
  grammar, parsing, links, layout paths, mode filtering, rules-only fallback,
  every validator failure class, air/ground reach, text formatting and the
  digest's lack of match state.
- New files are free of analyzer diagnostics in a Debug `-warnaserror` build.
  The same build reports pre-existing diagnostics in other files.
- Lint parity: each branch run was compared with a detached `main` worktree on
  the same machine, and the warning multisets are identical.
  - Classic, `world-war-iii` profile: 2 warnings, identical to `main`.
  - Classic, `ai-assistant-only` profile: 193 warnings, identical to `main`,
    and the same pre-existing `^ReusableMindController` error as `main`.
  - RA2, all maps, integrated data-only RA2 with this chrome: 795 warnings,
    identical to `main`.
  - TS (shares the common main menu): 135 warnings, identical to `main`.
  - D2K: 128 warnings, identical to `main`.
  - The historic 11,543 World War III and 793 RA2 counts were recorded
    earlier; today's `main` gives the numbers above.
- Catalog validation passes for Classic (10 factions) and RA2 (12 factions)
  with zero issues.
- Native screenshots were reviewed for Classic and RA2 at 1280×720 and
  1920×1080, including:
  - navigation from the main menu and from Factions & Capabilities;
  - keyboard navigation.
## United States concept board ready for Checkpoint B review

### What We Built

- `docs/concept/usa/` now holds the roadmap gate-2 concept and silhouette
  review for the United States pack: the single roster board (all 21 actors
  from `faction-spec-usa.md` §3 at native scale on snow, temperate and desert
  terrain, in player blue and red, with role-paired stock references and a
  black-fill silhouette row), the mandatory `USMBT`/`M1A2S` and `USICV`/`ARAS8`
  comparisons on all three theatres with the §8.1/§8.3 deltas called out,
  eight-facing and state sheets, a player-colour zone board, and a silhouette
  overlap matrix against every actor in the contract's §7 matrix. Every board
  ships at native scale and as a 3x nearest-neighbour copy, with a manifest.
- The README explains how to read the boards, maps each Checkpoint B
  requirement to its evidence, records a scale check against stock and shipped
  references, gives per-actor landmark and player-colour notes, and lists the
  open questions the product owner must settle before any sheet is produced.
- The contract and roadmap status lines now say the concept board is ready for
  product-owner review. Nothing is marked approved, and the contract's §9.1
  source-verification blocker is untouched.

### What We Fixed

- None in shipped content. Three art-audit observations are recorded in the
  README instead of being changed here: shipped `M1A2S`/`ARAS8` frames are about
  55% player colour through accidental remap-ramp quantization; the shared
  low-poly renderer culls against a camera mirrored from its projection; and
  the `SAHINX`/`F15SA` wings face downward and are culled.

### Key Decisions

- The boards are generated, not painted: original low-poly geometry in the
  product repository is rendered, quantized into indexed Red Alert frames with
  authored player-colour zones on the remap ramp, and drawn with a port of
  `PlayerColorRemap`, so the review shows real sprite pixels.
- Stock Red Alert art (and upstream sprites derived from it) appears only as
  flat scale ghosts or black silhouettes, and terrain is synthesized from the
  clear-tile index statistics, so the repository does not redistribute
  Westwood artwork. Project-generated modern-faction art is shown in colour.
- The U.S. material colour is a cool forest green, the existing modern-faction
  camera is kept, and player colour follows the contract zones strictly (4–26%
  of pixels). Each is listed as an open question rather than silently settled.
- `MH60` is added to the `USUH60` silhouette comparison because it is a
  buildable Allied Black Hawk missing from the contract's overlap matrix.

### Verification

- Engine worktree built with `dotnet build -c Release -p:TargetPlatform=win-x64`:
  0 warnings, 0 errors (used for `OpenRA.Utility` extraction only).
- Boards generated by the product generator at commit `6ce701d`; a second,
  independent run into a fresh scratch directory produced byte-identical PNGs
  (SHA-256 compared for all ten files). Total size ≈ 3.9 MB.
- Every board was inspected at native scale and at 3x. Product renderer tests:
  47 passed (roster equals §3, eight unique unclipped facings per state,
  intentional remap share, reserved palette indexes unused, state changes
  alter the outline, deterministic output, remap port sanity).
- Documentation-only change: no rules, sequences, Experience, catalog, lint
  baseline or engine test is affected.

## Engine synced with upstream OpenRA bleed (2026-09-14)

### What We Built

- `codex/upstream-sync` brings the fork up to upstream `bleed` 9ea513cb11 with
  real merge commits; no rebase or squash, and every fork feature is kept.
- The previous "Merge upstream OpenRA bleed" (16f4f873b2, 2026-08-19) was a
  single-parent squash of upstream ef5541cce4..a520984d91 (122 commits), so git
  still treated the fork as diverged on 2026-01-27. A plain merge of `bleed`
  replayed those 122 commits and produced 31 conflicts, several of them add/add
  on files that already matched upstream. The sync is therefore three merges:
  1. 934e39704a records a520984d91 as merged with the tree unchanged. Before
     committing, a normal three-way merge of a520984d91 was checked to produce
     this branch's exact tree on every clean path; its 10 conflicts were all
     fork-only additions (UTC DateTime comment, `RandomFactionMemberOf`,
     headless ScreenShaker guard, bot `Additional*Types`, AspNetCore launcher
     reference, fork projects in the slnx, `launch-game.cmd`, AI hotkeys,
     Dynamic Environment checkbox) that the squash had already resolved.
  2. 41801ac21a merges up to 82ae93f3ea, just before the float2/float3 move.
  3. aaa54c5dc7 merges `origin/bleed`. With the corrected merge base only 4
     textual conflicts remained.
- Upstream brought (54 new commits): System.Numerics Vector2/Vector3 instead of
  float2/float3, SIMD Color, faster WAngle trigonometry, interleaved blend-mode
  batching in SpriteRenderer (plus the normal-blend restore and blend-span flush
  fixes), texture subdata uploads for sheets, span-based FieldLoader/MiniYaml
  parsing, QuickSave hotkey, map-preview hiding and Discord toggles, validation of
  stored map generator options, "no bot types" lobby guards, radar tick
  optimisation, and eleven new d2k missions (Atreides 06a/06b/07/08/09a/09b,
  Ordos 06b/07/08/09a/09b) with the Harkonnen 04 fix and campaign Hunt timeout.

### Fixed

- Conflicts: `FieldLoader.ParseDateTime` keeps the fork's UTC round-trip note
  with upstream's span parsing. `SpriteFont.Measure` keeps the fork's
  `UnicodeText.PrepareForDisplay` (Arabic shaping/bidi) before upstream's span
  line split; the fork's font fallback and every DrawText shaping call merged
  cleanly into upstream's Matrix3x2 rotation. `SpriteLoader` keeps System.Linq
  for headless placeholder frames. The common in-game `GLOBAL_KEYHANDLER` runs
  both the fork's `AIHotkeyLogic` (five AI keys) and upstream's
  `QuickSaveHotkeyLogic` (KP_ENTER, no collision with any AI key).
- Fork-only code adapted to upstream APIs (it compiled before only through the
  removed float2/float3 types, their implicit int2 conversions, or the old
  ITexture/Sheet API):
  - `OpenRA.Platforms.Null/NullPlatform.cs` (`ITexture.SetSubData`)
  - `OpenRA.Game/Graphics/CursorManager.cs` (faction cursor effects)
  - `OpenRA.Game/Graphics/SpriteLoader.cs` (headless `DummySpriteFrame`)
  - `OpenRA.Mods.Common/Traits/World/EnvironmentDirector.cs`
  - `OpenRA.Mods.Common/Widgets/EarthMapPreviewWidget.cs`,
    `Widgets/Logic/EarthMissionStudioLogic.cs`,
    `Widgets/Logic/Editor/EarthAIEditorLogic.cs`
  - `OpenRA.Mods.Common/Widgets/FactionPackPreviewWidget.cs`
  - `OpenRA.Mods.RA2/Graphics/ArcRenderable.cs`, `Graphics/TintedCell.cs`
  - `OpenRA.Mods.RA2/Traits/Render/WithCarrierParentPipsDecoration.cs`,
    `WithMindControllerPipsDecoration.cs`
- Headless runs without installed content (the fork's RL/validation mode) now
  work for d2k. Before this branch a headless d2k match crashed on its first
  render tick on the unmerged fork as well: ShroudRenderer's WorldLoaded is
  skipped on missing art, leaving null layers. `RenderShroud` now returns when

  which keeps the gameplay-relevant `BuildableTerrainLayer`; without it the
  first concrete hit crashed at about tick 4,900.

### Key Decisions

- Recorded the earlier squash as a merge instead of re-resolving 31 spurious
  conflicts; this is truthful (the tree already contained those commits) and
  gives future upstream merges the right base.
- Did not sweep the Debug analyzer diagnostics. `make check` on unmerged main
  already reports 177 of them, all in 54 fork-added or fork-modified files; no
  file identical to upstream has one. The merge leaves exactly the same
  multiset. Clearing them means editing files that parallel workstreams own
  (companion and RL bridges, AI widgets, the RA2 library), so it is left for a
  dedicated follow-up once those land.
- Warning baselines differ from older notes because they predate later fork
  commits, not because of this merge: World War III lint is 2 warnings (the
  11,543 latent warnings were fixed by RA2 fusion 4/N) and every remaining
  count includes the 2 untranslated feedback-button tooltips added on
  2026-09-15 (AI Assistant Only 191 + 2 = 193, RA2 793 + 2 = 795). The AI
  Assistant Only lint error on Straits Shield is also pre-existing and is not
  changed here.
