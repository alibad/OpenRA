# 2026-09-28

## Native Faction Catalog

### What We Built

- The main menu now has a **Factions** entry, and each faction pack in
  Factions & Capabilities has an **Open in Faction Catalog** button. Both open
  a native catalog of the loaded game (Classic and RA2) that lists only that
  mode's factions: modern factions first, then the original countries, with a
  note naming catalog factions that exist only in another mode.
- For each faction it shows:
  - the title, tagline, doctrine, summary and signature units;
  - the roster grouped by domain, with production icons and tags for signature
    and unique units;
  - unit details read from the loaded rules: cost, build time, HP, armor,
    speed, sight, power, transport, attack reach, roles, counters,
    prerequisites and each weapon's range, damage, reload and targets.
- "More on the web" and "Unit on the web" open the public
  `rtsai.net/{factions|units}/{id}?mode=` pages through `Renderer.TryOpenUrl`.
- Keyboard:
  - Left/Right: faction.
  - Up/Down: unit.
  - PageUp/PageDown: details.
  - Enter: web page.
  - Esc: back.
- The panel is 1200×680 at 1280×720 and 1440×900 at 1920×1080. Long text
  uses native multiline tooltips and scroll panels. `docs/faction-catalog.md`
  has the code map and the catalog location contract.
- `OpenRA.Utility <mod> --check-faction-catalog` validates the shared catalog
  against the loaded rules and Experience faction packs. It can also export the
  rules digest that the main menu, the catalog and the in-match companion HUD
  post to the companion.

### What We Fixed

- Saudi Arabia's roster was missing its fixed-wing airfield `SAFLD`, which only
  Saudi Arabia can build. The new check found it.

### Key Decisions

- Every number comes from the loaded rules, and rosters and exclusivity come
  from a static reading of the tech tree. The product catalog is editorial
  only.
- The panel ships in common chrome but appears only where a mod declares it.
  Other mods that share the common main menu hide the button and keep their
  layout.
- Air reach is judged against both Classic `AirborneActor` and RA2 `Air`
  target profiles. The existing observation classifier, `ObservationWeaponTargets`,
  only recognizes the literal `Air`. That classifier is not changed here; see
  the product report.

- New files are free of analyzer diagnostics in a Debug `-warnaserror` build.

  - TS (shares the common main menu): 135 warnings, identical to `main`.

  - The historic 11,543 World War III and 793 RA2 counts were recorded

  - navigation from the main menu and from Factions & Capabilities;
  - keyboard navigation.
## United States concept board ready for Checkpoint B review

### What We Built

- `docs/concept/usa/` now holds the roadmap gate-2 concept and silhouette
  review for the United States pack: the single roster board (all 21 actors
  from `faction-spec-usa.md` §3 at native scale on snow, temperate and desert
  terrain, in player blue and red, with role-paired stock references and a
  black-fill silhouette row), the mandatory `USMBT`/`M1A2S` and `USICV`/`ARAS8`
  comparisons on all three theatres with the §8.1/§8.3 deltas called out,
  eight-facing and state sheets, a player-colour zone board, and a silhouette
  overlap matrix against every actor in the contract's §7 matrix. Every board
  ships at native scale and as a 3x nearest-neighbour copy, with a manifest.
- The README explains how to read the boards, maps each Checkpoint B
  requirement to its evidence, records a scale check against stock and shipped
  references, gives per-actor landmark and player-colour notes, and lists the
  open questions the product owner must settle before any sheet is produced.
- The contract and roadmap status lines now say the concept board is ready for
  product-owner review. Nothing is marked approved, and the contract's §9.1
  source-verification blocker is untouched.

### What We Fixed

- None in shipped content. Three art-audit observations are recorded in the
  README instead of being changed here: shipped `M1A2S`/`ARAS8` frames are about
  55% player colour through accidental remap-ramp quantization; the shared
  low-poly renderer culls against a camera mirrored from its projection; and
  the `SAHINX`/`F15SA` wings face downward and are culled.

### Key Decisions

- The boards are generated, not painted: original low-poly geometry in the
  product repository is rendered, quantized into indexed Red Alert frames with
  authored player-colour zones on the remap ramp, and drawn with a port of
  `PlayerColorRemap`, so the review shows real sprite pixels.
- Stock Red Alert art (and upstream sprites derived from it) appears only as
  flat scale ghosts or black silhouettes, and terrain is synthesized from the
  clear-tile index statistics, so the repository does not redistribute
  Westwood artwork. Project-generated modern-faction art is shown in colour.
- The U.S. material colour is a cool forest green, the existing modern-faction
  camera is kept, and player colour follows the contract zones strictly (4–26%
  of pixels). Each is listed as an open question rather than silently settled.
- `MH60` is added to the `USUH60` silhouette comparison because it is a
  buildable Allied Black Hawk missing from the contract's overlap matrix.

### Verification

- Engine worktree built with `dotnet build -c Release -p:TargetPlatform=win-x64`:
  0 warnings, 0 errors (used for `OpenRA.Utility` extraction only).
- Boards generated by the product generator at commit `6ce701d`; a second,
  independent run into a fresh scratch directory produced byte-identical PNGs
  (SHA-256 compared for all ten files). Total size ≈ 3.9 MB.
- Every board was inspected at native scale and at 3x. Product renderer tests:
  47 passed (roster equals §3, eight unique unclipped facings per state,
  intentional remap share, reserved palette indexes unused, state changes
  alter the outline, deterministic output, remap port sanity).
- Documentation-only change: no rules, sequences, Experience, catalog, lint
  baseline or engine test is affected.
